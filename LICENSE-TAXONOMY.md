## What the taxonomy is

The taxonomy is the pitfall rules and their text, wherever they appear:

- the rule files in `src/taxonomy/**/*.yaml`,
- the generated module `src/taxonomy/data.ts` and its compiled copies in `dist/`,
- the rules as served by the Data Pitfalls MCP connector, the `datapitfalls`
  CLI and npm package, and the pages of avoidingdatapitfalls.com.

## In plain English

- **Using the rules at work is free.** Checking your own charts, queries,
  dashboards and reports with the connector, the CLI or the website, including
  at your job, is the use this license is meant to allow.
- **Sharing is free, with credit.** You may copy, quote and adapt the rules for
  non-commercial purposes if you credit *Avoiding Data Pitfalls* by Ben Jones
  (Wiley, 2019) and link to <https://www.avoidingdatapitfalls.com>.
- **Selling needs a license.** Selling the rules, or building them into a paid
  product, service, course or training, needs a separate commercial license from
